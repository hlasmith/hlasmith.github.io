Work

Projects and My Delivery Approach.

I adapt to every project, client and team. Here's what stays constant.

Approach

01

People first, process second

Frameworks serve the team, not the other way around. I get to know how people work best and build delivery around them.

02

Transparency and honesty

I say what needs saying. If something isn't working or a timeline is at risk, the client hears it from me early.

03

Continuous improvement

Always looking for a better way. Process, tooling, communication, ways of working. If it can be improved, I want to find it.

04

Data-informed decisions

Cycle time, throughput, capacity, trends. When you have evidence, you have better conversations and better decisions.

05

Innovation and curiosity

Genuinely curious about how things can be done differently. I push for innovation where it adds real value, not just for the sake of it.

06

Leading by example

I set the standard I expect. If I'm asking the team to keep the board updated, mine is updated first.
